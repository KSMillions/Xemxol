Core Competencies

Technical Expertise

Our consulting team brings hands-on, field-tested expertise across the full civil engineering project lifecycle — combining technical rigour with practical site knowledge.

Engineering Software Proficiency

In-depth working knowledge of AutoCAD, Civil 3D, HEC-RAS, QGIS, EPANET, Primavera P6, and other relevant design and planning tools used in civil and water engineering.

AutoCAD Civil 3D HEC-RAS QGIS Primavera P6

Road Design & Stormwater Management

Concept design, road design, drainage design, hydrology management, and quantity measurement across provincial, rural and urban environments. Surface modelling, geometric design, and road sign/marking adjustments.

Road Design Drainage Stormwater Geometric Design

Hydrological & Hydraulic Analysis

Evaluation and analysis of hydrological and hydraulic structures for 1:10, 1:20, and 1:50 year flood scenarios. Assessment of structure overtops, scour conditions, and provision of detailed intervention recommendations for drainage infrastructure.

Flood Analysis Hydraulic Modelling Scour Assessment Drainage Structures

Design Preparation & Checking

Preparation and checking of engineering designs, detailed drawings, structural estimates, plans and specifications for engineering projects. Proficiency in producing construction drawings, measurements and Bills of Quantities (BOQ) in accordance with client and legal requirements.

Design Drawings BOQ Specifications Estimates

Site Management & Team Leadership

Expertise in overseeing daily site activities and managing logistics to ensure construction work progresses according to plan. Proven ability to lead, motivate and manage diverse teams of engineers, contractors, and subcontractors to achieve project goals effectively.

Site Supervision Logistics Team Leadership Subcontractor Management

Technical Reporting & Documentation

Ability to write clear and concise technical reports, proposals and documentation. Supporting project administration through attendance of site meetings, issuing of documents, and updating drawings. Contract administration including site handovers and issuing construction documentation.

Technical Reports Proposals Contract Administration Site Handovers

Regulatory Compliance & Safety

In-depth understanding of construction safety standards, regulations and procedures to maintain a safe working environment for all personnel. Familiarity with local building codes, zoning regulations and all other legal requirements to ensure projects are completed in full compliance with applicable laws.

Safety Standards Building Codes Zoning Regulations Legal Compliance

Project Planning, Procurement & Tendering

Project Planning Site Inspections Procurement Tendering

Water Engineering Wastewater Infrastructure Assessment Rehabilitation
